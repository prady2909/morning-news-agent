SummaryA miracle technology does not guarantee a miraculous business, as impressive and useful innovations often attract many competitors, leading to oversupply and price collapses. This pattern, seen in industries like airlines, railways, oil, and fiber optics, ultimately benefits consumers through lower prices but frequently leads to the failure of many producers.
